Cleaning (scaling & polishing)

Plaque hardens into tartar that brushing can't shift. It stains teeth, irritates gums and can cause bad breath. A professional clean lifts it off and leaves teeth feeling smooth.

  • Quick visit that fits around your day
  • Helps with stains, bleeding gums and breath
  • Easy habit to keep up every six months or so
Ask about cleaning
Fix it early

Fillings

A small cavity is easiest to fix when it's small. We clean out the decay and fill the space so the tooth is strong again and the pain, if there was any, goes away.

  • Numbing first so you stay comfortable
  • The decayed part is cleaned out and filled
  • Advice on how to stop it coming back
Ask about fillings
Tooth pain? Start here

Root canal treatment

When the inside of a tooth gets infected or badly inflamed, it throbs, reacts to hot and cold, or swells. A root canal cleans out the infection so you can keep your own tooth.

  • Local anaesthesia, so you're comfortable throughout
  • The infected inside is cleaned and sealed
  • Often followed by a crown to protect the tooth
Ask about root canal

Tea, chai and time all leave their mark. Whitening lightens stains so your smile looks brighter and fresher. First we check your teeth are healthy enough for it.
